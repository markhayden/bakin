# Easter Dessert Roundup: Five Spring Picks

## 1. Lemon Lavender Macarons

Light, floral, and just sweet enough. The lavender buttercream filling is subtle — more herb than perfume. Allow 2 days for making and maturing.

**Difficulty:** Advanced | **Prep:** 3 hours + overnight rest

## 2. Carrot Cake with Brown Butter Cream Cheese Frosting

Classic carrot cake elevated with browned butter in the frosting. The toasted milk solids add a caramel depth that pairs perfectly with warm spices.

**Difficulty:** Intermediate | **Prep:** 90 minutes

## 3. Strawberry Shortcake Trifle

Individual trifle cups layered with vanilla pound cake, macerated strawberries, and whipped cream. Simple enough for kids to help assemble.

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Prep:** 30 minutes

## 4. Pastel Meringue Nests

Crispy meringue shells with a marshmallow center, tinted in pastels and filled with lemon curd and fresh berries. Impressive for almost no effort.

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Prep:** 20 minutes active + 2 hours baking

## 5. Hot Cross Bun Bread Pudding

Turn day-old hot cross buns into a spiced bread pudding with vanilla custard. Best served warm with a drizzle of cream.

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Prep:** 15 minutes + 45 minutes baking

## Shopping List

Most ingredients overlap. Buy once: heavy cream, eggs, butter, lemons, strawberries, vanilla, powdered sugar. Specialty: dried lavender, meringue powder, lemon curd.
