# No-Knead Artisan Bread

Crusty on the outside, soft and airy inside. Start the dough the night before.

## Ingredients

- 3 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp instant yeast
- 1 1/2 cups warm water (110F)

## Method

1. **Mix** flour, salt, and yeast in a large bowl. Add water and stir until a shaggy dough forms. No kneading.
2. **Rest** covered with plastic wrap at room temperature for 12-18 hours. Dough will be bubbly and doubled.
3. **Shape** by turning dough onto a floured surface. Fold in thirds like a letter, then fold the other direction. Place seam-side down in a parchment-lined bowl. Cover and rest 1 hour.
4. **Preheat** oven to 450F with a Dutch oven inside for 30 minutes.
5. **Bake** by dropping parchment and dough into the hot Dutch oven. Cover and bake 30 minutes. Remove lid and bake 10-15 more minutes until deep golden.
6. **Cool** on a wire rack at least 20 minutes before cutting.

## Notes

- For sourdough flavor, reduce yeast to 1/4 tsp and extend the first rise to 24 hours.
- Add 1/2 cup olives, sun-dried tomatoes, or roasted garlic at the mixing stage for variations.
