# Sunrise Smoothie

A layered tropical smoothie that looks as good as it tastes. The trick is blending each layer separately and pouring slowly over the back of a spoon.

## Bottom Layer (Mango)

- 1 cup frozen mango chunks
- 1/2 cup orange juice
- 1 tbsp honey

Blend until completely smooth. Pour into a tall glass, filling about one-third.

## Middle Layer (Strawberry)

- 1 cup frozen strawberries
- 1/4 cup coconut milk
- 1 tbsp honey

Blend until smooth. Slowly pour over the back of a spoon onto the mango layer to keep the colors separate.

## Top Layer (Berry)

- 1/2 cup frozen mixed berries
- 1/4 cup Greek yogurt
- Splash of milk

Blend until smooth. Layer on top using the spoon technique.

## Finishing

- Top with a few fresh berries, a slice of orange, or a sprinkle of granola
- Serve with a wide straw so you get all three layers in each sip

## Meal Prep Tip

Pre-portion the frozen fruit for each layer into separate bags. Morning assembly takes under 5 minutes.
