# Million Dollar Bacon

Sweet, spicy, and impossibly addictive. The brown sugar caramelizes into a crackly shell while the cayenne sneaks up on you.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb thick-cut bacon
- 1/2 cup dark brown sugar, packed
- 2 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp freshly cracked black pepper
- 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 375F. Line a sheet pan with foil, then place a wire rack on top.
2. Mix brown sugar, maple syrup, black pepper, cayenne, and paprika in a shallow bowl.
3. Press each bacon strip firmly into the sugar mixture, coating both sides generously.
4. Lay strips on the rack in a single layer. Sprinkle any remaining sugar mixture on top.
5. Bake 20-25 minutes, rotating the pan once at the halfway mark. Bacon is done when the sugar is bubbling and the edges are deeply caramelized.
6. Let cool on the rack for 5 minutes. The coating firms up into a candy-like shell as it cools.

## Serving

- Brunch board centerpiece alongside scrambled eggs and biscuits
- Chopped over pancakes or waffles
- Crumbled onto a salad for a sweet-savory crunch
- Straight off the rack, standing in the kitchen, pretending you're just "checking if it's done"
