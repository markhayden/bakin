# Food Blog Redesign

## Goal

Modernize the food blog with better recipe cards, faster load times, and improved SEO. The current layout works but looks dated and doesn't render well on mobile.

## Key Changes

- **Recipe cards:** Structured data with jump-to-recipe, print-friendly layout, and ingredient scaling
- **Image optimization:** Serve WebP with JPEG fallback, lazy load below-the-fold images
- **SEO:** Add schema.org Recipe markup, improve internal linking between related recipes
- **Navigation:** Category pages for cuisines, difficulty levels, and seasonal collections
