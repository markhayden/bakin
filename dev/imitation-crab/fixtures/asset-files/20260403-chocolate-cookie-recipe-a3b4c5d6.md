# Double Chocolate Chunk Cookies

Rich, fudgy cookies with pools of melted chocolate. Makes 24 cookies.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup butter, browned and cooled
- 1 cup dark brown sugar
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup Dutch-process cocoa powder
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 8 oz dark chocolate, chopped into chunks
- Flaky sea salt for finishing

## Method

1. Brown butter in a saucepan until nutty and amber. Cool to room temperature.
2. Whisk browned butter with both sugars until smooth. Add eggs and vanilla, whisk until glossy.
3. Fold in flour, cocoa, baking soda, and salt until just combined. Fold in chocolate chunks.
4. Refrigerate dough at least 2 hours (overnight is better).
5. Scoop 2-tablespoon portions onto parchment-lined sheets. Leave 2 inches between cookies.
6. Bake at 350F for 10-12 minutes. Centers should look slightly underdone.
7. Sprinkle with flaky salt immediately. Cool on pan 5 minutes before transferring to a rack.

## Why Brown the Butter

Browning adds toasted, nutty depth that plain melted butter can't match. The milk solids caramelize, creating hundreds of new flavor compounds. Worth the extra 5 minutes every time.
