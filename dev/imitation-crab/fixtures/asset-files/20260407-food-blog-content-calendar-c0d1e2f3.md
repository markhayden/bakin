## Content Goals

- **Frequency:** 2-3 posts per week
- **Mix:** 60% recipes, 20% guides, 20% roundups/lists
- **Photography:** Each recipe post needs a hero image (pixel) and at least 2 process shots
- **SEO:** Target one long-tail keyword per post (e.g., "no knead bread recipe overnight")

## Social Distribution

- Instagram: Hero image + carousel of process shots, publish same day as blog
- Pinterest: Vertical pin with recipe title overlay, schedule for peak hours (8pm MT)
- Newsletter: Weekly digest every Sunday with links to that week's posts
