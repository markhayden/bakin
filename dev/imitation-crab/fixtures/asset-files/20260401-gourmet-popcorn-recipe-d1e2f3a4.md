# Gourmet Popcorn Three Ways

## Base Popcorn

- 1/2 cup popcorn kernels
- 3 tbsp coconut oil
- Fine sea salt to taste

Pop kernels in coconut oil over medium-high heat in a heavy-bottomed pot. Shake frequently until popping slows to 2 seconds between pops. Transfer to a large bowl.

## Rosemary Parmesan

- 2 tbsp melted butter
- 1 tbsp fresh rosemary, minced
- 1/3 cup finely grated Parmigiano-Reggiano
- Pinch of garlic powder

Drizzle butter over warm popcorn. Toss with rosemary, parmesan, and garlic powder. Season with salt.

## Smoky Chili Lime

- 2 tbsp melted butter
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp chili powder
- Zest of 1 lime
- Squeeze of lime juice

Toss warm popcorn with butter, spices, and lime zest. Finish with a light squeeze of lime juice.

## Honey Sriracha

- 2 tbsp melted butter
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp sriracha
- 1/2 tsp sesame seeds

Whisk butter, honey, and sriracha. Drizzle over popcorn and toss. Top with sesame seeds.
