# 🌽 Grilled Street Corn & Black Bean Power Bowl

## Recipe

**Serves:** 2 | **Time:** 25 minutes | **Calories:** ~420 per bowl

### Ingredients

**For the bowl:**
- 2 ears fresh corn (or 1½ cups frozen corn, thawed)
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans, rinsed and drained
- 1 cup cooked brown rice or quinoa
- 1 cup shredded purple cabbage
- 1 avocado, sliced
- ½ cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- ¼ cup fresh cilantro, roughly chopped
- 2 limes, cut into wedges

**Street corn dressing:**
- 3 tbsp plain Greek yogurt (sub: light sour cream)
- 1 tbsp mayonnaise (just 1 — don't skip it, it matters)
- 1 tsp chili powder
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- ¼ tsp garlic powder
- Pinch of cayenne
- Salt to taste
- Juice of 1 lime

**Optional toppings:**
- 2 tbsp crumbled cotija or feta cheese
- Tajín for sprinkling
- Jalapeño slices

---

### Steps

1. **Char the corn.** Grill ears directly over a flame (gas burner or grill) for 8–10 minutes, rotating until kernels are charred in spots. Alternatively, broil on high 5–7 minutes. Let cool slightly, then cut kernels off the cob. If using frozen corn, dry it well and char in a cast-iron skillet over high heat — no oil — until you get color.

2. **Make the dressing.** Whisk together Greek yogurt, mayo, chili powder, smoked paprika, garlic powder, cayenne, lime juice, and salt. Taste and adjust — it should be tangy, smoky, and a little spicy.

3. **Warm the beans.** Heat black beans in a small pan with a pinch of cumin and salt, or microwave for 60 seconds. Simple.

4. **Build your bowl.** Start with rice or quinoa as the base. Add beans, charred corn, purple cabbage, cherry tomatoes, and avocado.

5. **Dress it.** Drizzle the street corn dressing generously over the top. Don't be shy.

6. **Finish.** Top with cotija or feta, fresh cilantro, a squeeze of lime, and a dusting of Tajín. Jalapeños if you want heat.
