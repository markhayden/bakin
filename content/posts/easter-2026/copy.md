## Recipe

### Spring Herb Salmon with Lemony Asparagus & Whipped Ricotta
*Serves 4 | Ready in 30 minutes*

This is Easter on a plate. Pan-seared salmon with a fresh herb crust, tender roasted asparagus, and a cloud of whipped lemon ricotta that makes everything feel like spring arrived ahead of schedule.

It's the kind of dish that looks like you spent hours — and takes thirty minutes. Perfect for Easter brunch, Easter dinner, or honestly any Sunday in April when you want to feel a little fancy.

Light, nourishing, and absolutely stunning.

---

### Ingredients

**For the Herb-Crusted Salmon:**
- 4 salmon fillets (6 oz each), skin-on
- 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
- ¼ cup fresh dill, finely chopped
- ¼ cup fresh flat-leaf parsley, chopped
- 2 tbsp fresh chives, chopped
- Zest of 1 lemon
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt & pepper

**For the Lemony Asparagus:**
- 1 bunch asparagus, woody ends trimmed
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Juice of ½ lemon
- ¼ tsp garlic powder
- Salt & pepper
- Optional: shaved Parmesan or toasted pine nuts

**For the Whipped Lemon Ricotta:**
- 1 cup whole-milk ricotta
- Zest of 1 lemon + 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 small garlic clove, grated
- Salt & white pepper
- Fresh dill to finish

**To Serve:**
- Lemon wedges
- Edible flowers or microgreens (optional but gorgeous)
- Flaky sea salt
- Extra fresh herbs

---

### Instructions

1. **Whip the ricotta.** Blend ricotta, lemon zest, lemon juice, olive oil, garlic, salt and white pepper in a food processor or with a hand mixer until silky smooth. Taste, adjust. Refrigerate until serving.

2. **Prep the asparagus.** Toss asparagus with olive oil, lemon juice, garlic powder, salt and pepper. Roast at 425°F for 10–12 minutes until tender with slight char at the tips.

3. **Make the herb crust.** Mix dill, parsley, chives, and lemon zest together on a plate.

4. **Sear the salmon.** Pat dry, season with salt and pepper. Heat olive oil in a cast-iron skillet over medium-high. Add salmon skin-side up, sear 3 minutes. Flip, brush tops with Dijon, press herb mixture onto each fillet. Cook 3–4 more minutes. Skin will be crispy, fish just opaque.

5. **Assemble.** Swipe a generous spoonful of whipped ricotta across each plate. Lay asparagus alongside. Place salmon on top. Finish with lemon wedge, microgreens or edible flowers, flaky salt.
