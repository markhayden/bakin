## Healthy Eating Tip

**Why Kale?**

Kale is one of the most nutrient-dense foods on the planet — and it's easier to love than you think.

**Key Nutritional Facts:**
1. **Vitamin K powerhouse** — One cup of raw kale delivers over 680% of your daily Vitamin K needs, essential for bone health and blood clotting.
2. **Antioxidant-rich** — Packed with quercetin and kaempferol, two potent antioxidants linked to reduced inflammation and lower risk of chronic disease.
3. **Surprising Vitamin C source** — Cup for cup, kale has more Vitamin C than an orange, supporting immune function and collagen production.

**Why it matters:** Most of us are chronically under-eating dark leafy greens. Kale bridges that gap efficiently — a single serving covers a wide spectrum of micronutrients your body actually needs.

**Easy tip:** Massage your kale. Seriously. Drizzle with a little olive oil and work it with your hands for 60 seconds. It breaks down the tough fibers, making it sweeter, softer, and way more enjoyable to eat. Game-changer.
