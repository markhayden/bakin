## Recipe

### Crispy Baked Chicken & Protein Waffles
*Serves 2 | Ready in 35 minutes*

All the soul-food satisfaction of chicken and waffles — without the deep fryer or the food coma. We're baking the chicken to crispy perfection and sneaking Greek yogurt and oat flour into the waffles for a protein punch that actually keeps you full.

Sweet, savory, and smarter than it looks.

---

### Ingredients

**For the Crispy Baked Chicken:**
- 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (or thighs)
- ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
- 1 tsp hot sauce (Frank's or your fave)
- ½ cup panko breadcrumbs
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp onion powder
- ¼ tsp cayenne
- Salt & pepper to taste
- Olive oil spray

**For the Protein Waffles:**
- 1 cup oat flour (blend rolled oats if needed)
- ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
- 2 eggs
- ½ cup milk of choice
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 tbsp honey or maple syrup
- Pinch of salt
- ½ tsp vanilla extract

**For the Hot Honey Drizzle:**
- 3 tbsp honey
- ½ tsp red pepper flakes
- ½ tsp apple cider vinegar

**Optional Toppings:**
- Fresh thyme or rosemary
- Flaky sea salt
- Sliced green onion
- A little extra hot sauce

---

### Instructions

**Chicken:**
1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Line a baking sheet with a wire rack.
2. Mix Greek yogurt and hot sauce in a bowl. Add chicken and coat well. Marinate 10 min (or overnight).
3. Mix panko with garlic powder, smoked paprika, onion powder, cayenne, salt & pepper on a plate.
4. Press each chicken piece firmly into the panko mixture to coat all sides.
5. Place on the wire rack, spray lightly with olive oil.
6. Bake 22–25 minutes until golden and cooked through (internal temp 165°F). Broil last 2 min for extra crunch.

**Waffles:**
1. Whisk together oat flour, baking powder, and salt.
2. In a separate bowl, whisk Greek yogurt, eggs, milk, honey, and vanilla.
3. Fold wet into dry — don't overmix. Batter will be thick.
4. Cook in a preheated, lightly oiled waffle iron until golden (~4–5 min).

**Hot Honey:**
1. Warm honey in a small saucepan over low heat. Add pepper flakes and ACV.
2. Stir 1 min. Remove from heat. Done.

**Assemble:**
- Waffle on the plate. Chicken on top. Drizzle hot honey generously. Finish with flaky salt and fresh thyme.

## Nutrition Education Sidebar
*(Optional second post or Stories content)*

**Why this works nutritionally:**
- **Greek yogurt (in both chicken marinade + waffles):** Adds protein, tenderizes the chicken naturally via lactic acid, keeps waffles moist without excess fat
- **Oat flour:** Higher fiber than white flour, slower-digesting carbs = more sustained energy
- **Baking vs. frying:** Saves ~15–20g of fat per serving without sacrificing crunch (wire rack = air circulation = crispy)
- **Hot honey:** Honey provides quick-release energy; capsaicin from pepper flakes has mild metabolism-boosting and anti-inflammatory effects
